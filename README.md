# Google Developer Group WOW Pune

Welcome to the official repository for Google Developer Group Women of Wonders (WOW) Pune!

## About GDG WOW Pune

GDG WOW Pune is a community-led, inclusive tech group focused on empowering women and underrepresented genders in technology through learning, networking, and collaboration. As part of the global Google Developer Groups ecosystem, we organize events, workshops, and initiatives that foster diversity and inclusion in the tech community.

## Our Mission

- Foster an inclusive tech community in Pune
- Provide learning opportunities for developers at all levels
- Create a supportive network for women and underrepresented genders in tech
- Share knowledge about Google technologies and open-source projects
- Bridge the gender gap in the technology sector

## Activities & Events

- Technical workshops and hands-on sessions
- Speaker series featuring industry experts
- Hackathons and coding challenges
- Study jams and codelabs
- Networking events and community meetups
- Mentorship programs

## Get Involved

There are many ways to get involved with GDG WOW Pune:

- **Attend Events**: Join our upcoming events and connect with the community
- **Speak**: Share your knowledge and experience as a speaker
- **Volunteer**: Help organize and run our events
- **Mentor**: Support others on their tech journey
- **Sponsor**: Help us create impactful events and programs

## Code of Conduct

We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free and inclusive event experience for everyone regardless of gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disabilities, neurodiversity, physical appearance, body size, ethnicity, nationality, race, age, religion, or other protected category. We do not tolerate harassment of event participants in any form.
